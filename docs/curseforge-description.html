Quest tracker for WoW Forever
Clean Quest Tracker
See what is left in your zone and who in your party is on the same quest, without opening the quest log.
Works with Questie Free and open source
🗺️
Quests by zone
Your current zone stays on top. Fold zones one at a time, pin focus to keep only the zone you are in open as you travel, or expand and collapse everything in one click.
🔍
Search New
Click the magnifying glass and type part of a quest title or objective. The list narrows as you type and the matching letters are highlighted. Folded zones with a match open by themselves.
👥
Party questing
A small badge shows how many group members also have each quest. Hover it to see who, click it to share the quest with the people missing it.
⚔️
Objective progress
Bars fill as you collect items and defeat enemies. Finished quests turn green and fold down to a single line.
🎨
Yours to arrange
Compact or Comfortable spacing, your own colours, six party badge styles and an optional dark panel. Unlock the frame to move or resize it, and choose how much it animates, or turn animation off.
🛡️
Behaves like the original
Quest item buttons, map pins, the right-click menu and shift-click chat links all work as they do in Blizzard's tracker, and the add-on never modifies Blizzard's tracker code.
See it in the game
Quests grouped under their zone, current zone first.
The party badge on a quest another member has.
The settings window. Hover any option to see what it changes.
🧭
Getting started
Install, then click the cog on the tracker's header (it appears when you hover) or type /cqt. Hover over any setting to see the part of the tracker it changes.
🤝
Works with
Questie gives party quest counts at any distance through its party sync.
Spoken adds its voiceover play buttons to your quests.
If another add-on shows its own quest tracker, turn that one off so your quests don't appear twice. Clean Quest Tracker hides Blizzard's tracker for you; switch that off in its settings if you want it back for scenarios or achievements.
