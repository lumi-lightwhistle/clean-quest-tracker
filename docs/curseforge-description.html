Quest tracker for WoW Forever
Clean Quest Tracker
See what is left in your zone and who in your party is on the same quest, without opening the quest log.
Works with Questie  ·  Free and open source
   Quests by zone
Your quests sit under their zone, with the zone you're in on top. The list re-sorts as you travel, so what's nearby is always first.
▸Click a zone heading to fold it.
▸Click the pin to keep only the zone you're in open.
▸Click the arrows to step through every zone open, everything folded but your focused quest, and every zone folded.
▸Turn zones off for one plain list, if you prefer.
The zone tools appear when you hover the Quests bar.

   Search  NEW
Hover the Quests bar, click the magnifying glass and type part of a quest title or objective. The list narrows as you type and the matching letters are marked.
▸Capitals don't matter, and the count shows how many quests match.
▸Folded zones with a match open by themselves.
▸Press Enter to keep the filter while you play.
▸Esc while typing, or the X, clears it and puts your zones back the way they were.
▸Mark matches with a strip or by colouring the letters, in a colour you choose.
   Party questing
A badge beside each quest shows how many of your group also have it.
▸Hover it to see who has the quest and how far along they are.
▸Click it to share the quest with anyone missing it.
▸Works across the map when you both run Questie.
▸Six badge styles, as an icon or as text.
Each member's progress, in their class colour.
   Objective progress
Bars fill as you collect items and defeat enemies. Finished quests turn green and fold down to a single line.
▸Choose where finished quests sit in each zone: as tracked, at the top or at the bottom.
▸Pick the bar colour and thickness, or turn bars off.
▸A quest glows briefly the moment it's ready to hand in (unless animation is off).

   Getting started
Hover the tracker's header and click the cog, or type /cqt. Hover any setting to see the part of the tracker it changes, shaded in gold. Settings that don't apply right now are greyed out and say why.
Shift-click a quest to link it in chat. Right-click it for the quest menu, including Stop tracking.
The settings window, on the Layout page.
   Works with
Questie gives party quest counts and progress at any distance through its party sync. Spoken adds its voiceover play buttons to your quests.
If another add-on shows its own quest tracker, turn that one off so your quests don't appear twice. Clean Quest Tracker hides Blizzard's tracker for you; switch that off in its settings if you want it back for scenarios or achievements.
Behaves like the original
Quest item buttons, map pins and the right-click menu work as they do in Blizzard's tracker, and shift-click links a quest in chat even with the chat box closed. The add-on never modifies Blizzard's tracker code.
